Privacy Policy

At JIRO (“we,” “our,” or “us”), your privacy is a top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit and use our website and tools.

Information We Collect

  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics (pages visited, device/browser type) to improve performance.
  • Input Data: Information you enter into our tools is processed to deliver the tool's function and is not stored beyond the session unless you choose to save it.
  • Contact Info: If you contact us, we collect your email and name only to respond.
  • Cookies: Minimal cookies for sessions and light analytics; no cross-site tracking.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our tools and website.
  • Personalize user experience and analyze usage patterns.
  • Respond to inquiries and send essential communications.
  • Protect the site and users from abuse or attacks.

Data Security & Retention

We implement strong security practices. Whenever possible, sensitive inputs are processed client-side in your browser. Contact information is retained only as required to fulfill communications or legal obligations.

Your Rights

  • Access and correct personal data where applicable.
  • Request deletion of personal data where feasible.
  • Opt-out of non-essential communications.
  • Withdraw any given consent at any time.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services for hosting and analytics. We do not share personal user content with third parties except when required by law or with your explicit consent.

Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will update the effective date and post the revised policy here.
